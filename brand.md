# Brand

## Name and what we do

**Matrix Root** (MatrixRoot) is an engineering-first software solutions studio and talent incubator based in Hyderabad, India. We design and deliver digital products, platforms, and backend systems while helping student founders and emerging engineers build production experience.

## Audience

Technical founders, ambitious startups, growing organizations, and emerging student engineers looking for dependable, lean software delivery and collaborative sprint mentorship.

## What we promote

- **Custom Product Engineering:** Web apps, SaaS platforms, backend architectures, APIs, and AI integrations (https://matrixroot.tech/services).
- **Talent Incubator:** Guided production sprints for student founders and builders (https://matrixroot.tech/incubator).

## The feeling

**Technical, premium, bold, and energetic.** High-craft motion that feels engineered, physical, and intentional—never decorative fluff or cheap template transitions.

## Assets

- **Colours:**
  - Primary Flame Orange: `#F26522` / `#ff5f03` (accent, active state, focal highlights)
  - Deep Amber Flame: `#c2410c` (hover, deep emphasis)
  - Pure White: `#ffffff` (surface, elevated cards)
  - Porcelain Void: `#f8fafc` / `#EFEFEF` (light ground contrast)
  - Charcoal Slate: `#0f172a` / `gray-900` (ground, primary ink, headings)
  - Muted Slate: `#475569` / `gray-600` (secondary copy, descriptions)
  - Light Slate Border: `#e2e8f0` (hairline dividers, ghost outlines)
- **Fonts:** System sans-serif (`Geist`, `-apple-system`, `system-ui`, `sans-serif`), Mono (`Geist Mono`, monospace)
- **Logo:** `public/logo-transparent.png`, `public/logo.png`, Dark circle with "MR"

## Never

- Never use generic floating gradient blur blobs with no physical intent.
- Never use jarring spring overshoots on text; text reveals must be clean, masked, or locked to a baseline.
- Never show fake numbers or lorem ipsum; all stats must carry clear context or an "illustrative" badge.
- Never snap elements into place—every entrance and exit must follow an eased curve.
- Never use slow linear fades when a shape morph or staged displacement tells the story better.
